# Undocx

**AI writes it, you steer it.** Undocx is a minimal document editor where AI drafts from your brief and you reshape the result one paragraph at a time, in your own voice, grounded in your own sources.

It is built for people who write documents that have to be right: proposals, reports, policies, updates, letters. Nothing is rewritten that you did not ask to change.

## How writing works

1. **Describe it.** Say what you are writing in a sentence or two. Pick a writer profile, a voice, a rule set and any sources you want to use.
2. **Answer a few questions.** Undocx asks only what it cannot already tell from your picks, such as price, dates or length. Skip any of them.
3. **Outline when it helps.** Turn the outline step on for long documents, or let Undocx decide.
4. **Get a first draft.** Written from your brief, answers, voice, rules and sources, with citations.
5. **Steer it.** Rewrite a single paragraph with an instruction, regenerate a section, compare variations, or ask the assistant about the document. Every change shows a diff and can be undone in one step.

## Features

### Editor
- Rich text with headings, lists, quotes, tables, columns, rules, task lists and page breaks
- **Locked text**: lock a paragraph and no rewrite, regenerate or variation will touch it
- Page setup for paper size, margins and fonts
- Focus mode, command palette and keyboard shortcuts
- Notes to self, attached to a passage or to the whole document
- Autosave

### AI assistance
- **Brief chat** with follow-up questions and an optional outline
- **Rewrite** a selection or a block, **regenerate** a section, or compare **variations** and keep one or merge them
- **Assistant** conversation per document, aware of what you have written and of your profile, voice and rules
- **Claims check** that flags statements in a draft that your sources do not support
- **House rules**: spelling variant (British, American, Canadian, Australian), banned words, formatting rules and custom rules, checked against your draft

### Your context
- **Writer profiles**: who you are, your role, your organisation and your readers
- **Voices**: learned from samples of your own writing
- **Rule sets**: reusable spelling, wording and formatting rules
- **Sources**: files (PDF, Word, text, Markdown, CSV), pasted text and links, attached to documents and cited in the draft

### Versions
- Named versions you save yourself, plus automatic ones before and after AI changes
- Restore any version, with a safety copy of what you had

### Library of documents
- Search by title and text
- Sort by recent edits, newest, title or length
- **Folders** and **tags** to organise, with filters and pagination

### Ask your documents
Search everything you have written in one place and get an answer with a citation for each claim. Undocx finds the passages that matter across all your documents and answers only from them. If the answer is not there, it says so. Every chat is saved, so you can reopen earlier ones from the history list or delete them.

### Export
Word, OpenDocument, PDF, Markdown, HTML and JSON. Citations become footnotes or a references list, and locked text exports as ordinary text. Nothing in an exported file depends on Undocx.

## Your data

- Your documents are used only to produce the text you ask for, and the AI provider is told not to store requests. Your content is never used to train models.
- Sign in with Google. Undocx stores your name, email address and profile photo from Google.
- Search works by splitting your documents into passages and storing numerical embeddings of them for your account only.
- Delete a document, a source or your whole account at any time. Account deletion removes your documents, uploaded files, library, search index, chat history and usage records straight away, and backups within 30 days.

See the in-app [privacy](https://undocx.app/privacy) and [terms](https://undocx.app/terms) pages for the details.

## What Undocx does not do

- No real-time collaboration, shared workspaces or teams. One person per document.
- No review links or comments from other people.
- It does not write without you: every change is something you asked for, and locked text is never touched.
